Install and start

  1. Sign in and obtain trial or purchase access through the official Avery Logic Works website.
  2. Download and extract the complete Windows package.
  3. Open QuadraHydra.exe and review the product terms.
  4. Use the activation credential issued for your trial or purchase when required by the build.

Privacy and connectivity

Process information and local workload data are intended to remain on the Windows computer. Licensing and trial validation may send limited account, entitlement, or device-fingerprint information to Avery Logic Works systems so access can be validated and misuse can be detected. The product may also make ordinary HTTPS requests for public version/update information.

Important process-control warning

Save your work before changing processes. Raising priorities, suspending, or ending a process can interrupt programs or cause unsaved work to be lost. Administrator privileges do not make an action safe, and performance results depend on the computer and workload.
