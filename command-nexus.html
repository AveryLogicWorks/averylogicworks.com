What it is

One place to use AI — and to make your own.

This build comes with starter AI agents, so you can just open it, pick one, and start your AI discovery. Prefer to build your own? Make a custom agent instead. The choice is yours.

Key features

Take command of what your agents can do for you.

👁

Visibility Window

See exactly what your agent is doing, the moment it does it. When an agent runs outside the Nexus, it works inside a safe environment with measures in place to keep its activity ethical and under control.

⚙

AI Creation Engine

Pick your use case, set capabilities with simple sliders, and dial in personality-trait levels — building an agent is as easy as making choices, not writing code.

🧠

AI-Assisted Knowledge Infuser

Just tell the AI what you want your agent to know, and your AI makes it so. No data wrangling, no setup files — describe it, and it's done.

🚀

Starter AI Agents

Open the Nexus, pick a ready-made starter agent, and begin right away. Starters are there for free use — choosing one never stops you from building your own.

🛡

Safe & Ethical by Design

A built-in governance system is there to keep the Nexus safe and ethical for everyone who uses it.

✨

No Coding Required

Built for almost everyone. Use and make AI without writing a single line of code — the power is yours whether you code or not.
