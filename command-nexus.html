Fused Search Registry

Starter AI Agents

Apex Glaux & External Intelligence (Dim 4)

An open fourth dimension in the Trifecta Fold. Apex Glaux — a proprietary Avery Logic Works portable cognitive engine — plugs in as a fully trusted second brain with its own memory, relations, frontier cognition, and response synthesis. Third-party API or local models can also plug in, but are treated as untrusted: confidence is capped, all output is screened through guardrails, and a circuit breaker disables them after repeated failures. External models provide a slight capability boost (e.g., online search via API) — they can never override Command Nexus's own intelligence.
