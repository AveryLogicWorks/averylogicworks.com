What it is

One place to use AI — and to make your own.

Command Nexus is the place where a user can open ready-made agents, create custom agents, tune their capabilities, connect tools and APIs, and keep the whole system visible enough to understand what the agents are doing.

Key features

Command Nexus capabilities that point to real systems, not placeholder cards.

The public page now matches the build files you handed me: governed agent creation, local adaptive memory, model/backend selection, approval-gated tools, audit logging, key activation, and customer-safe support AI.

⚙

AI Forge / Creation Engine

Create and shape agents by use case, capabilities, traits, guardrails, and knowledge instead of starting from raw code. This is the front door for building customer, research, coding, writing, tutor, workflow, and business agents.

🧠

Local Adaptive Memory

Each AI can keep its own local memory file, retrieve recent memories, search by keyword, and use local Ollama embeddings when available. The memory layer is built to work locally first.

🔗

Model & API Runtime Bridge

Command Nexus can route work through the configured backend path, including local Ollama, OpenAI-compatible API use, Brave Search support, capability aliases, local tools, and a model registry for matching tasks to models.

👁

Visibility, Approval & Audit

Risky actions are meant to pass through human approval, registry checks, and audit logging before the agent does anything system-changing. Users get a command center instead of a blind autonomous box.

🛡

Baseline Guardrails

The safety floor is coded as an always-active guardrail layer. It blocks illegal, harmful, deceptive, exploitative, and dangerous requests before normal AI processing continues.

🔑

License Keys & Field Codes

Public plans use signed license keys, while field distribution can use Hermes Codes through the Moirai Ledger / Prometheus Activation path. Owner-only and internal authority stay separate from customer tiers.

💬

Customer AI Layer

A restricted public-facing customer AI can answer pricing, support, billing, product, complaint, refund, and technical questions while refusing to reveal protected internal mechanics.

🛠

Capability & Upgrade Path

The build notes include expanded capabilities, upgrade categories, parental control expansion, and premium feature paths so the product can grow without pretending every future option is already unlocked for every user.

Before you start

Disclaimers & parental controls.

Active development notice

Command Nexus is offered through full access plans. Core product features are presented as working and available, while licensing, updates, security, and stability may continue to improve over time. Features, limits, availability, and pricing can change as the product is updated. See the pricing & price-change terms.

Trial & account terms

A free website account is required. The free trial is tied to your account and is meant as a genuine trial — not permanent free access. Trial length and renewal rules may change over time. Keys are issued per account; sharing, reselling, or abusing keys may result in access being paused or revoked.

If access is paused or revoked, the event will be reviewed before any final action is taken. We understand that life happens — sometimes in the worst way possible, sometimes when you least expect it, and sometimes when you are not even conscious enough to expect it. A child may press keys. A phone may butt-dial. Inputs may be repeated accidentally in ways that look wrong but were not intentional. If something looks accidental, it will be reviewed as accidental. The activity pattern, what was done, and how it was done may be examined to tell the difference between a real violation and an accident — because we have seen proof that life happens, and it does not always happen in the easiest way.

AI safety & content notice

Command Nexus lets you use and build AI agents. AI output can be inaccurate, unexpected, or unsuitable for some audiences. You are responsible for how you configure and use your agents. A built-in governance system is designed to keep activity within safe, ethical bounds, but no automated system is perfect — review what your agents do.

Parental controls & younger users

Command Nexus is intended for adults. If a minor uses it, a parent or guardian should set up and supervise the account and is responsible for that use. We recommend:

  • Adult-owned account: keep the account, email, and key under a parent or guardian, and keep keys private.
  • Supervised sessions: review the agents a minor creates and the tasks they run — use the Visibility Window to watch agent activity.
  • Device-level controls: use your operating system or browser parental controls (e.g. Windows Family Safety, screen-time and content limits) to manage access and time.
  • Stop access anytime: sign out, or contact us to pause or remove access tied to your account.

Questions about access, trials, safety, or parental controls? Email Averylogicworks@gmail.com.
