Privacy Policy

What the site collects, why it collects it, and how purchase records may be used.

This policy explains the site’s handling of account information, email preferences, operational analytics, service-intake records, purchase-related logs, and third-party services used to operate Avery Logic Works.

1. Information you provide

Account, contact, and request details

If you create an account, join updates, make a purchase, submit a private service request, donate, subscribe, or contact the site, Avery Logic Works may collect the information you provide directly, such as your email address, display name, newsletter preference, supporter-update preference, billing-related contact details, and any message contents or project details you send.

2. Information collected automatically

Visit, session, and payment-related activity

The site may record operational data such as page visits, referrers, browser or device signals, visitor/session identifiers, account creation events, login events, settings changes, service-intake activity, checkout-start events, successful payment events, refunds, disputes, and related system timestamps used for security, debugging, fraud review, and business operations.

3. Why the information is used

Running the site, fulfilling purchases, and reviewing billing issues

  • To create and maintain accounts
  • To confirm email ownership and support password resets
  • To honor newsletter and supporter-email preferences
  • To operate supporter, donation, subscription, or service-purchase flows
  • To log purchase and fulfillment records connected to a transaction
  • To monitor site activity, performance, misuse, and abuse
  • To review duplicate charges, refunds, cancellations, and disputes
  • To respond to support, billing, or policy questions
4. Email and mailing services

Newsletter messages are preference-based.

If you opt into newsletters or future supporter updates, your email may be sent to a mailing service provider to deliver those messages. Newsletter and supporter communications are intended to remain optional, and future emails should include an unsubscribe path where appropriate.

5. Third-party providers

Some infrastructure is handled by outside services.

The site may use third-party providers for hosting, authentication, database storage, mailing operations, media delivery, analytics, and payment processing. Examples in the current build include Supabase for account, database, and function infrastructure, EmailOctopus for newsletter operations once enabled, and Stripe or other processors when support or purchase features are live.

6. Payments

Payment-card details are usually handled by the processor, while the business keeps transaction records.

When support, subscriptions, or purchases are processed through a payment provider, full payment-card details are typically handled by that processor, not stored directly by the public site code. Avery Logic Works may still receive and retain billing-related records such as transaction identifiers, payment status, customer email, charge, refund, or dispute references, and other records needed to confirm transactions, review refunds, respond to disputes, and manage supporter or service access.

7. Data retention

Information may be kept as long as it remains useful or necessary.

Account records, email preferences, service-intake submissions, purchase logs, refund and dispute records, security logs, and operational analytics may be retained as long as needed for site operation, legal compliance, fraud prevention, billing review, security review, dispute response, or project continuity, unless a shorter retention period is required by law.

8. Security

Reasonable safeguards may be used, but no system is perfect.

Avery Logic Works may use practical technical and organizational safeguards to reduce misuse, unauthorized access, accidental exposure, billing abuse, and payment-related fraud. Even so, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Your choices

You can update preferences or ask questions about account and billing records.

You can update account preferences through the site where available, unsubscribe from mailing communications when those tools are active, or contact the support or billing address for questions about account status, communication preferences, or purchase-related records.

10. Data we will never sell or share

Your private information stays private.

Avery Logic Works does not and will not sell your data to anyone. We do not share your personal information with other companies except where required to complete a purchase you initiated, or where a third-party service is directly connected to the Avery Logic Works ecosystem (such as the payment processor for your transaction, the authentication provider for your account, or the mailing service for newsletters you opted into). We are dedicated to keeping your private information private — not as a marketing slogan, but as a founding commitment.

11. Command Nexus & product data collection

What Command Nexus collects and why.

Command Nexus does not collect clipboard information, browsing history, personal files, or background device data. The only data that may be collected from Command Nexus activity is limited to:

  • License key activation events — when a key is activated, the key itself, the account it is tied to, and the timestamp, so access can be verified and managed.
  • Flag events — if the built-in governance system flags activity that may violate safety rules, the flagged action, what triggered it, and the context around it may be logged so the event can be reviewed.
  • Accidental or unusual activity — if something looks accidental (for example, a child pressing keys, unintended repeated inputs, or device-level mishaps like pocket or butt presses), the activity pattern, what was done, and how it was done may be reviewed to determine whether it was intentional or accidental. This is not about spying on you — it is about telling the difference between a real violation and life happening.

If an event is reviewed and determined to be accidental, it will not be treated the same as an intentional violation. The purpose of collecting this information is to protect both the user and the platform — so that access is not unfairly revoked when life happens in the worst way, at the worst time, or in ways nobody expected.

12. Policy updates

This page may be revised as the site grows.

As the platform expands, privacy practices may change. Updated policy language becomes effective when posted unless another date is stated on the page.
