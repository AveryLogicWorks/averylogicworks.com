How it works

Direct to the founder.

  • Every bug report goes directly to the founder's private vault — no ticket queue, no support tier.
  • Include as much detail as you can. Steps to reproduce are especially helpful.
  • Your name and email are optional, but including them means you can get a follow-up when the fix is ready.
  • Critical issues are prioritized immediately.
